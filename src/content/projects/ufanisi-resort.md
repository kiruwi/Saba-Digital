# Ufanisi Resort Food Delivery App

## Project Overview

As a self-proclaimed foodie, I'm naturally drawn to food-related topics. For my first Google UX Design Certificate project, I chose to redesign the Ufanisi Resort food delivery app, transforming it from a problematic interface to an intuitive, user-friendly experience for busy residents of Kisii, Kenya.

<!--StartFragment-->

This case study documents my complete design process, from initial research and identifying pain points to creating wireframes, prototypes, and the final polished interface.

<!--EndFragment-->

## The Challenge

In today's fast-paced world, many people struggle to find time to cook or visit restaurants. I set out to solve this everyday problem: how can busy people in Kisii get delicious food from Ufanisi Resort without disrupting their packed schedules?

## My Design Process

1. **User Research** - Conducted interviews and surveys to understand user needs and pain points
2. **Competitive Analysis** - Studied existing food delivery apps to identify best practices and opportunities
3. **Information Architecture** - Created logical flow and navigation structure
4. **Wireframing** - Developed low-fidelity designs to test core functionality
5. **Prototyping** - Built interactive prototypes for user testing
6. **Usability Testing** - Gathered feedback from real users to refine the design
7. **Visual Design** - Applied brand identity and visual elements to create the final UI

## Features

* Streamlined 3-tap ordering process
* Multi-language support (English & Swahili)
* Voice search capabilities
* Saved preferences & quick reordering
* Payment flexibility (mobile money & cash)
* High-contrast accessibility design
* Smart upsell recommendations
* Real-time order tracking
